The perfect companion for Hitster - the music quiz card game!

DukeStar makes playing Hitster seamless and fun:

• SCAN - Point your camera at any Hitster card QR code
• LISTEN - Hear 30-second song previews instantly in-app
• GUESS - Try to name the year, artist, and song title
• SCORE - Track your game progress with built-in scoring

FEATURES:
- Instant QR code scanning with camera
- In-app 30-second song previews via Deezer
- Full song playback in Deezer, Spotify, or YouTube
- Unique "flip phone" gesture to start playback
- Game score tracking across sessions
- Beautiful neon cyber theme
- Support for 308 Hitster cards

Perfect for game nights with friends and family!

Note: This app requires Hitster game cards (sold separately).
